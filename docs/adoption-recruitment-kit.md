# Adoption study recruitment kit

Use this neutral copy to recruit engineers for the [Scout adoption study](adoption-study.md). It describes the time commitment and data handling without naming the products or previewing the expected result. Do not send it until the facilitator has confirmed the date, environment, and compensation (if any).

## Invitation

**Subject:** Research interview and Go browser automation exercise

I'm looking for engineers who have operated browser automation from a Go service, worker, or agent harness during the past year. The study has a short interview about your current workflow and a hands-on comparison of two Go browser automation approaches using a local test application. You do not need prior experience with either product.

The session takes about 2 hours 45 minutes; please reserve a 3-hour block for transitions and breaks. It uses a supplied machine or a clean, isolated development environment and a deterministic local fixture; please do not bring production credentials or customer data. With your consent, I will record the screen for research review. You may decline recording, skip any question, or stop at any time. Notes will use a participant code; contact details, if needed for scheduling, will be kept separately and removed after follow-up. Any quotation will be used only with separate permission.

If you are interested, please complete the short eligibility questions below. Participation is voluntary. [Add scheduling link/contact and compensation details here.]

## Eligibility screener

Ask these questions before scheduling. Do not describe Scout or the recovery hypothesis.

1. In the last 12 months, have you operated browser automation from a Go service, worker, or agent harness?
   - Yes, in production
   - Yes, in a pilot or internal tool
   - Evaluated it but did not deploy
   - No
2. What browser automation stack did you use or evaluate? Free text.
3. Did you own implementation, deployment, or on-call support for it? Select all that apply.
4. Can you reserve a 3-hour block for a session that takes about 2 hours 45 minutes, using a local test application and ordinary Go development tools?
5. Do you agree to participate in a research session? Yes / No.
6. Optional: may the facilitator record your screen? Yes / No. A no answer does not exclude you.

Prior Scout experience is not required. Avoid recruiting only from Scout users or people already briefed on its product thesis. Record the eligibility answers and selection rationale separately from the de-identified session record.

## Facilitator checklist

- Confirm the participant meets the recent-operator criterion or is a relevant evaluator who rejected deployment; keep these groups identifiable in analysis.
- Confirm the 3-hour block for the approximately 2-hour-45-minute session, breaks, accommodation needs, and any compensation before booking.
- Do not reveal product names or recovery claims before the interview and task instructions call for them.
- Ask recording permission separately; conduct the session without recording when declined.
- Keep name, email, scheduling notes, and consent record outside the repository and participant JSON.
- Use the existing neutral [interview protocol](adoption-study.md#interview-25-minutes), [task card](adoption-task-card.md), and [session record](adoption-session-template.md). Counterbalance product order.
- After the session, preserve the participant checkout and source evidence with a participant code; review for credentials and personal data before storage or sharing.
- Do not enter a participant result unless the session occurred. Rehearsals remain excluded by the evidence audit.
